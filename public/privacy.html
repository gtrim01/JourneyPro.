JourneyPro Privacy Policy

Effective 10 August 2026 · Published by Innovative Apps

The short version: JourneyPro doesn't collect your personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and nothing about you is sent to our servers. Your trips and rig details stay on your own device.

Information we do not collect

JourneyPro does not ask for, collect, or transmit personal information. There is no registration, no user account, no advertising, and no analytics tracking in the app. We never request access to your device's location, contacts, camera, or files.

Information stored on your device

Your saved trips, vehicle and caravan selections, and settings are stored locally on your device using standard browser storage. This information never leaves your device and is not visible to us. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's data or your browser's site data for JourneyPro.

Third-party services the app talks to

Weather (Open-Meteo): when you request weather, the app sends the map coordinates of the towns and roadhouses on your planned route to the free Open-Meteo forecast service. These are the locations of public places you selected — not your device's location, which the app never accesses.

Hosting (Vercel) and content delivery: like every website, loading JourneyPro means your device makes standard web requests to our hosting and style/font providers, which may log routine technical data such as IP address and browser type to deliver and secure the service.

External links: "Map, photos & reviews" links open Google Maps, which is governed by Google's own privacy policy once you're there.

Children

JourneyPro is a general-audience trip-planning tool and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If JourneyPro adds features that change how data is handled (for example, optional accounts that sync trips between devices), this policy will be updated first and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to Innovative Apps via the developer contact details on JourneyPro's Google Play listing.
